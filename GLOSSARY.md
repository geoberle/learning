# Glossary

## Context

Personal learning through focused missions, teaching materials, and evidence of understanding.

## Language

**Mission**:
A concrete capability to acquire, grounded in a personal reason and bounded by observable success criteria.

**Workspace**:
The enduring home of one mission’s materials and learning history, spanning multiple learning tasks and sessions.

**Lesson**:
A short teaching unit focused on one concrete step toward a mission.

**Learning task**:
A question, exercise, or knowledge gap that warrants separate tracking within a mission.

**Learning record**:
Evidence of understanding, prior knowledge, or a corrected misconception that informs future teaching.

**Availability**:
Whether learning material has been published and can be used.

**Learning progress**:
Advancement toward a mission supported by the learner’s report or evidence from teaching; publishing material alone does not establish it.
